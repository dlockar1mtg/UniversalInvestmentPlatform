# Universal Investment Platform — Project Charter

## Mission

Build a personal unified investment intelligence platform for Devon Lockard that consolidates investment data, portfolio state, forecasts, risk, recommendations, transactions, and performance across asset domains.

The platform exists to improve long-term net-worth growth through explainable, evidence-based, risk-aware decisions.

## Covered domains

UIP is designed to support:

- Metals
- Crypto
- Magic: The Gathering
- Stocks and ETFs
- Acorns
- Housing
- Macro and recession indicators
- Cash and cash-equivalent instruments
- Additional approved asset domains

## Primary outcomes

UIP should:

- track authoritative holdings and transactions;
- maintain current and historical asset intelligence;
- recommend monthly capital allocation;
- evaluate existing holdings, new contributions, sales, reallocation, and cash;
- distinguish asset outlook from portfolio action;
- provide downside-aware forecasts and risk measures;
- explain why a recommendation changed;
- evaluate completed recommendations;
- support point-in-time historical simulation and model validation;
- make all decision-relevant results traceable to source evidence;
- remain available remotely from any authorized laptop.

## Decision actions

The universal action vocabulary may include:

- buy;
- increase;
- maintain;
- pause;
- reduce;
- exit;
- rotate;
- hold cash.

Recommendations must distinguish strategic and tactical actions where relevant.

## Portfolio decision standard

The monthly capital-allocation process should evaluate:

- planned contributions;
- current holdings;
- concentration;
- valuation and outlook;
- downside risk;
- liquidity;
- tax or transaction considerations where available;
- candidate purchases;
- candidate sales;
- capital rotation;
- cash retention.

The objective is to maximize empirically validated risk-adjusted growth within an explicit risk and drawdown budget.

## Core principles

### Explainability

Every certified or decision-relevant result must be explainable and traceable.

### Preservation

Original source data, transaction evidence, recovery artifacts, and certification evidence must be preserved according to policy.

### Stable contracts

Shared domain boundaries require governed contracts and coordinated activation.

### Reproducibility

Certified results must be reproducible from identified code, inputs, contract versions, and model versions.

### Transaction safety

Purchases and sales must post through an idempotent, transactional, immutable ledger process.

### Traceability

Recommendations, forecasts, performance, risk, and portfolio actions must retain source and model lineage.

### Domain separation

Source domains retain native responsibilities while UIP owns canonical portfolio integration.

### Comparable universal core

Cross-domain analytics use a comparable universal core with source-specific extensions where necessary.

### Advisory status

UIP is advisory only. Devon retains final decision authority and manually executes external trades or purchases.

## User authority

Devon retains final approval for:

- investment decisions;
- project scope;
- architecture;
- roadmap;
- paid services;
- production providers;
- destructive cleanup;
- recovery restoration;
- material contract policy;
- production acceptance.

## Non-goals

UIP is not intended to:

- autonomously execute trades;
- give third parties control of Devon’s accounts;
- conceal uncertainty;
- manufacture missing source data;
- silently remove unsupported assets;
- treat backtests as guaranteed future outcomes;
- depend permanently on one laptop.

## Charter principle

> UIP should help Devon understand what changed, what requires attention, where new capital should go, whether capital should rotate, and how prior decisions performed—while preserving uncertainty, evidence, and final human control.
