# Phase 5.2.3 — Universal Ranking Factor Engine

The factor engine converts a comparable Phase 5.2 input into eight explicit, auditable factor scores: decision score, confidence, action strength, risk-adjusted opportunity, liquidity, diversification contribution, portfolio capacity, and freshness.

Each factor retains its raw score, policy weight, weighted contribution, and explanation. The engine preserves Phase 5.1 score and confidence as separate concepts, rejects excluded inputs, validates comparability identity, and does not apply composite penalties or assign ranks. Those responsibilities begin in Phase 5.2.4.
