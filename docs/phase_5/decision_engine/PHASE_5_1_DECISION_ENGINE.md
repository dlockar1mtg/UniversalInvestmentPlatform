# Phase 5.1 — Universal Decision Engine

## Purpose

The Universal Decision Engine converts standardized intelligence into
auditable investment decisions across every supported asset class.

The engine consumes:

- forecasts and forecast confidence;
- historical forecast reliability;
- risk-adjusted opportunity measures;
- market-regime intelligence;
- portfolio diversification context;
- liquidity and execution quality;
- valuation or relative attractiveness;
- data-quality assessments;
- portfolio capital and exposure constraints.

The engine produces a standardized decision containing:

- eligibility;
- action;
- decision score;
- confidence;
- maximum permitted allocation;
- supporting reasons;
- supporting evidence;
- policy violations;
- policy and scoring versions;
- lifecycle and expiration information.

## Phase 5.1 build sequence

1. Decision contracts and domain models
2. Eligibility and data-quality gates
3. Universal decision scoring
4. Action classification
5. Confidence aggregation
6. Constraint and policy evaluation
7. Decision explanation and evidence
8. Batch decision orchestration
9. Serialization and audit outputs
10. Phase 5.1 certification

## Design principles

- Asset-class neutral
- Explainable
- Policy governed
- Deterministic by default
- Versioned
- Historically testable
- Auditable
- Safe under missing or unreliable data
