# Phase 5.3.3 — Opportunity Sizing Engine

This engine calculates independent feasible minimum, target, and maximum amounts
for each ranked opportunity. It combines ranking priority, confidence, and action
strength into an auditable conviction factor, then applies portfolio-gap,
opportunity-capacity, liquidity, deployable-capital, and purchase-minimum limits.

Sizing does not consume capital or resolve competition between opportunities.
Total suggested targets may exceed capital supply; Phase 5.3.6 will optimize that
competition after all constraints and objectives are established.
