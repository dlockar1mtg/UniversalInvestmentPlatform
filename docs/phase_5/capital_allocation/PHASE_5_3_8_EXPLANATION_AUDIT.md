# Phase 5.3.8 — Allocation Explanation and Audit Engine

This engine explains why every opportunity was funded, partially funded,
deferred, or excluded. It reports effective minimum, target, maximum, allocated
amount, unmet capacity, strongest utility drivers, binding constraints, funding
reason codes, and scheduled execution.

Each opportunity preserves six ordered audit stages: request, sizing,
constraints, objective, optimization, and execution. Immutable batch evidence
proves capital conservation and agreement between optimized purchases and the
execution plan without recalculating upstream results.
