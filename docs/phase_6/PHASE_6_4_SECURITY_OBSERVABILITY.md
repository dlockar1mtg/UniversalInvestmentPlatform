# Phase 6.4 — Security and Observability

Phase 6.4 introduces constant-time API-key authentication, role-derived permissions, reference-only secret configuration, and recursive sensitive-field redaction. A secured gateway applies authentication and authorization without weakening the stable v1 service contract.

Operational visibility includes correlated structured events, deterministic labeled counters and duration aggregates, plus independent liveness and dependency-aware readiness. Health failures are contained as evidence rather than escaping the operational boundary.
